Sindh Police · Sherlocks

The agents and how they work together

Two teams share one case board. The case-building team works in the background while the graph builds: it searches the police systems and reads FIR files, lab reports, CRO dossiers and uploads. The chat team talks with the officer in English, اردو or Roman Urdu. It answers exact questions from the board, investigates open ones, and asks what the case still needs.

The map

Two teams share one case board. Everything any agent finds goes onto the board with its source, and the knowledge base makes all of it searchable. The officer talks only to the Conversation agent.

Who does what

code always gives the same, exact answer. AI uses the Qwen model when it is available, with rules or templates as the fallback. live queries police systems, which is logged and budgeted.

What you can ask

"Danish kin cases mein hai?" · "kahan kahan FIR hui aur kab?"
Facts agent: each FIR with police station, date, crime in words and his role, and the count.
"koi khatarnak FIR?" · "qatl ka case hai?" · "gaari chori?"
Facts agent: the serious FIRs where he is accused, those where he is only the victim said apart, the lighter ones summarised.
"har FIR mein kya ilzaam hai?" · "FIR 391/26 ki tafseel"
Facts agent: per FIR, the crimes, his role, the complainant, the date, the accused, the complainant's own account and the status.
"IO ki report kya hai, guilty declare kiya?"
Facts agent: per FIR the IO's conclusion (challan sent to court, or closed as C class…), the IO's name and the report's own sentence.
"kitni mein convicted, kitni mein acquitted?"
Facts agent: "abhi tak kisi mein na saza hui na woh bari hua", then each FIR's status.
"imran ali danish ka kya lagta hai?"
Facts agent: the direct link first ("accused in FIR 345/26, which Danish filed"), then other routes, stated before inferred.
"danish ka kaam kya hai?"
Knowledge base, and if nothing is on record, the Research agent checks EVS, HOPE, HRMIS and PRVS and says so.
"wardaat Saddar mein hui, hamay Danish pr shaq hai"
Fact collector: incident place Saddar, Danish main suspect. Sherlock relates it to his record in one line.
📎 a CDR file
CDR agent: subscriber, contacts already on the graph, the incident day. Then the Questioner asks whose CDR it is.

Rules every agent follows

Numbers come from codeCounts and lists are computed by the Facts agent. The model only phrases them, and the Validator checks the numbers match.
Sourced or droppedEvery fact quotes its document or names its system. An answer that cites nothing real is replaced.
His role, alwaysEvery FIR is told with the person's role in it. Being the complainant or the victim is never presented as a charge.
About that person onlyA question about someone gets what concerns them; nothing about someone else is offered instead.
Missing, then looked upIf the records hold nothing, the Research agent checks the systems that would; if they hold nothing either, the reply says so.
Leads are markedStated, corroborated, inferred, speculative: inferred links are dashed and called leads.
Live calls are budgeted6 per question, 40 documents per run. Every call is in the audit log.
Works without the modelExact answers in plain sentences in the three languages. With the model, the same facts come in its own words.
